tizix on an IBM PC XT, in the browser

An 8088 at 4.77MHz with 256KB of memory, a CGA screen and a 360KB floppy drive, emulated by PCjs. The floppy is tizix's x86-ia16 image (make FDD=pcxt). It boots in about 15 seconds. Click the screen, then type: ls /bin, echo hello | cat, vi /root/a.txt (Esc, then :wq), ps, free. The keyboard is the US layout. Nothing is saved: reloading the page starts from the original disk.
